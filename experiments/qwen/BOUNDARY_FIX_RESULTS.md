# Outpainting boundary fixes — completed evidence, 2026-10-05

**No completed experiment is accepted as a seamless outpaint.** Lightweight controls and growing6 retain the guardrail defect. Native ControlNet40 has white outer regions; sparse/reference controls replace them with repeated panels. Localized40 produces one coherent scene but widens/displaces the rail; saved-top32 improves that join while source-border texture seams remain, with independent visual review pending. Both Fill50 prompts fail full composition. Exact source pixels and successful process exits are integrity results, not visual acceptance.

This overview consolidates saved local results. Track3 uses seed42, a 512×1152 canvas and the unchanged resized 512-square source at `(0,320,512,832)`. Preservation means that resized square is pasted exactly, not that the 720-square JPEG avoids resizing. Earlier six-step controls use Qwen-Image-2.1 Q4 with Viggle v0.2.1 r256 and real target growth. Completed ControlNet40 and Fill50 are full-canvas teachers; comparisons with those earlier controls are qualitative, not isolated model ablations.

MAE above is raw source reconstruction error in 0–255 units, before exact-source compositing. Luminance ratios, adjacent-row RGB jumps, rail angle and position are different diagnostics; they cannot be combined into a universal quality score. A small near offset can hide an elbow between incompatible directions. Failed feature extraction returns unknown measurements, not zero error. Source curvature, competing rail ridges/posts and fixed search corridors limit local rail fits.

The linked independent reviews retain full frames, untouched raw/composite crops, selected-feature overlays and comparison limits. Scene-prompt, ControlNet20/bridge40, [localized40 full comparison](boundary_eval/2026-10-05/controlnet-localized40-comparison/full-comparison.png) and [Fill50 composite](../flux1_fill/runs/2026-10-05/track3-50-seed42/composite.png) were also opened directly for this overview. Source-derived near fits differ from the earlier far-corridor measurement. Localized40's generated outer ridge is nearly parallel but displaced; a closer interior ridge has a different feature identity and cannot justify accepting the extra band. Color-only changes detector eligibility and yields an invalid fit, not successful geometry. Its `−17.404px` is horizontal displacement, not rail thickness.

Native40 and bridge40 isolate the bridge; sparse40 then isolates structural-channel support against bridge40. These matched pairs show that the tested unsupported structural field contributes to white blanks, while global scene failure persists. Reference40 changes reference input, prompt and prefix gating together. Localized40 retains its saved reference, prompt, noise, sparse context and schedule while changing completed-hint localization. Neither input nor hint sparsity removes transformer work: all completed40 teachers process 2,304 target tokens on every step; reference/localized40 also process 1,024 reference image tokens per step.

Growing6 forwards `[1280,1792,2304,2304,2304,2304]` target tokens: 12,288 per arm, 11.11% below a six-call full raster's 13,824. Future tokens are genuinely absent before activation, but all reference/text prefix work is recomputed in both chains; scale0 still executes ControlNet. This is actual compute geometry, not accepted streaming quality or iPhone timing. Saved-top32's [integrity audit](controlnet_runs/2026-10-05/track3-saved-top32-outpaint/independent_saved_top32_integrity.json) confirms unchanged retained inputs and only the 64-token hint difference. Its [frozen diagnostic](boundary_eval/2026-10-05/controlnet-top32-source-tangent/REPORT.md) supports local improvement without establishing full-image acceptance.

## Practical conclusion

Localized40 and its saved-top32 follow-up provide coherent scene continuation; saved-top32 makes a real local rail improvement, but remaining texture seams and pending independent visual review prevent promotion. Retain pure harmonic color as a possible final photometric adjustment after generation becomes structurally correct. Optical flow, softer masking, extra low-noise forwards and stronger text have not produced a complete fix. Acceptance requires the whole raw/composite output, both boundaries, correct feature identity and exact-source integrity together.

These are small local diagnostic controls, primarily one cover and seed; they do not establish failure of every configuration of the underlying methods. Timings are Mac M4 Max observations with load/decode, warm-up and phase differences. No iPhone benchmark, Player integration, mobile inference or live UI streaming is established.
